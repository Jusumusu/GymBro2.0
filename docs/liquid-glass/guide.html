Decide how much glass.

Solid: opaque, no blur. Subtle: 94% fill, 8px blur. Balanced: 80% fill, 16px blur. Expressive: 66% fill, 24px blur. Navigation, exercise selection, timer and menu chrome carry the material. Logging fields, charts and content stay readable on opaque surfaces. Reduced transparency and unsupported filtering use solid surfaces.

Try the interactions.

Start a fictional session, edit its fields, complete and undo a set, enter an invalid value, open Options, try Focus Mode, search and favorite a movement, or inspect the sample backup and updater controls. Changes exist only in memory. Secondary controls explain when they are outside the mock controller.

Verified scope.

Chromium: 960 layouts across six widths, 120 contrast pairs, logging and undo, menus, all four materials and the reduced-transparency fallback. No journal databases, localStorage, caches or service workers were created. The application source and offline asset hashes are unchanged. This is a visual study, with no real training calculations, backups or updates. Physical iPhone/Safari, installed PWA and final app performance checks remain for implementation after selection.
